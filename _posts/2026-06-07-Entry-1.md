In the early 2000s, I wrote programs for a school administrative system including attendance and health modules. 

After becoming the project engineer, my team developed very cutting-edge apps for teachers and school nurses. One, which was way before its time, tracked the H1N1 virus in the schools.

The health module also helped school systems to seamlessly receive medicaid funds each time a school nurse had an encounter with a medicaid eligible student.  These school systems were doing it manually and they had to hire a third-party biller to receive these funds.  This module allowed schools to keep 100% of all medicaid funds simply by doing it themselves.
